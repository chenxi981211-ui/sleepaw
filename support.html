Common questions常见问题

My alarm didn't ring闹钟没有响

On iOS 26 and later, Sleepaw uses real system alarms. Make sure you allowed Alarms when asked (Settings, Sleepaw, Alarms). Then open Sleepaw once so it can book your alarms again.在 iOS 26 及以上，枕边喵使用系统闹钟。请确认已允许「闹钟」权限（设置 → 枕边喵 → 闹钟），然后打开一次 App，让它重新预约闹钟。

What do the side buttons do when the alarm rings?闹钟响时按侧边键会怎样？

A side button snoozes while you have snoozes left. After that, or with snooze off, the alarm goes quiet for a minute and rings again. Tap "I'm up" to end it.还有贪睡次数时，按侧边键就是贪睡。贪睡用完或关闭贪睡时，闹钟会安静 1 分钟后再响，点「我起来了」才会结束。

How do I cancel my subscription?怎么取消订阅？

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Sleepaw. Cancel at least a day before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge.订阅由 Apple 管理。在 iPhone 上打开「设置」→ 点你的名字 →「订阅」→ 选择枕边喵。请在试用期或当前周期结束至少一天前取消，以免再次扣费。

I bought Sleepaw but it's locked on a new phone换了手机后会员不见了

Sign in with the same Apple ID, then tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen.请用同一个 Apple ID 登录，然后在订阅页面点「恢复购买」。

How do I delete my account?怎么删除账户？

In the app: Settings, My Account, Delete account. This removes your account and the data stored with it. If you have a subscription, cancel it with Apple too.在 App 里：设置 → 我的账户 → 删除账户。账号及其保存的数据会被删除。如果有订阅，也请在 Apple 那里取消。
