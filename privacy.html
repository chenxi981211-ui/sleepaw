Privacy Policy隐私政策

Last updated 22 November 2026最近更新：2026 年 11 月 22 日

In short: Sleepaw collects only what it needs to run your alarms, track your sleep and connect you with friends. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Who we are

Sleepaw (枕边喵) is made by Xi Chen, Zhenhao Peng and Hongxin Xu. Xi Chen, based in Brussels, Belgium, is responsible for your data. Contact: xicheninfo@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

Your account

Your email address, the name and profile picture you choose, and your language. Used to sign you in and show you to friends you add. If you sign in with Apple, Apple may give us a private relay email instead of your real one.

Alarms and sleep

Your alarms and their settings, the nights you sleep (bedtime, wake time, how long), the mood you log in the morning, your to-dos, points and items you unlock. Used to run the app, show your stats and give you tips.

Apple Health (optional)

If you allow it, Sleepaw reads and writes sleep data in Apple Health. It's used only to show your sleep in the app. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Motion and phone use, on your phone only

To work out when you really fell asleep, Sleepaw can use your phone's motion history (if you allow Motion & Fitness) and the times your phone was locked or unlocked while Sleepaw was running. This stays on your phone and isn't sent to us.

Friends and voice memos

The friends you add, your Rise Partner, and the voice memos you send or receive. Voice memos are stored on our servers and only shared with the person you send them to. To show a memo as text, its audio is sent to a speech-to-text service. What friends can see about you (for example your sleep or next alarm time) is up to you in Settings, Privacy.

Morning briefing

To read your briefing aloud in a natural voice, its text (such as the weather, your to-dos and a quote) is sent to a text-to-speech service, which returns the audio.

Weather

For the weather, Sleepaw estimates your rough location (city level) from your internet connection and asks a weather service for the forecast. We don't use GPS and don't store your location.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your card details. We receive whether your subscription is active, through our subscription provider.

Messages to support

If you write to us from the app, we receive your message, your email address and basic device information (app version, phone model) to help.

Services that process data for us

  • Supabase: account, database and file storage
  • RevenueCat: subscription status
  • ElevenLabs: the briefing's voice
  • OpenAI or Groq: turning voice memos into text
  • Open-Meteo, ipwho.is, ipapi.co: weather and approximate location
  • Resend: forwarding support messages to our inbox
  • Apple: sign-in, payments, notifications and Apple Health

They process data only to provide these services to us. Some of them are outside the European Economic Area. Where that's the case, transfers rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Legal bases (for users in the EU and UK)

We use your data to provide the service you asked for (contract), with your consent for optional features such as Apple Health, Motion & Fitness and notifications, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe and working.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your account and the data stored with it, except where the law requires us to keep something.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings, My Account, Delete account.
  • Turn off Apple Health, Motion & Fitness or notifications at any time in iPhone Settings.
  • Choose what friends can see in Settings, Privacy.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing xicheninfo@gmail.com.
  • In the EU you may also complain to your data protection authority. In Belgium, that's the Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données.

Children

Sleepaw isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is important.

我们收集什么，为什么

账号信息

你的邮箱、你设置的名字和头像，以及语言设置。用于登录，以及让你添加的好友看到你。如果用「通过 Apple 登录」，Apple 可能只给我们一个代理邮箱，而不是你的真实邮箱。

闹钟和睡眠

你的闹钟及设置、每晚的睡眠记录（入睡时间、起床时间、时长）、早上记录的心情、待办事项、积分和解锁的物品。用于运行 App、显示统计数据和给你建议。

Apple 健康（可选）

如果你允许，枕边喵会读取和写入 Apple 健康里的睡眠数据，只用于在 App 里显示你的睡眠。健康数据绝不会用于广告，也不会分享给任何人。

运动和手机使用情况（只保存在手机上）

为了判断你真正入睡的时间，枕边喵可以使用手机的运动记录（需要你允许「运动与健身」），以及枕边喵运行期间手机锁屏、解锁的时间。这些只保存在你的手机上，不会发送给我们。

好友和语音留言

你添加的好友、你的起床搭子，以及你发送和收到的语音留言。语音留言保存在我们的服务器上，只会给你发送的那个人。为了把留言显示成文字，音频会发送给语音转文字服务。好友能看到你的哪些信息（比如睡眠数据、下一个闹钟时间），由你在「设置 → 隐私」里决定。

晨报

为了用自然的声音朗读晨报，晨报的文字（比如天气、待办事项、每日一句）会发送给语音合成服务，再返回音频。

天气

为了显示天气，枕边喵会根据你的网络连接估算大致位置（城市级别），再向天气服务查询预报。我们不使用 GPS 定位，也不保存你的位置。

购买

付款由 Apple 处理，我们看不到你的银行卡信息。我们通过订阅服务商得知你的订阅是否有效。

联系客服

如果你在 App 里给我们留言，我们会收到你的留言、邮箱，以及基本的设备信息（App 版本、手机型号），以便帮你解决问题。

帮我们处理数据的服务

  • Supabase：账号、数据库和文件存储
  • RevenueCat：订阅状态
  • ElevenLabs：晨报的朗读声音
  • OpenAI 或 Groq：语音留言转文字
  • Open-Meteo、ipwho.is、ipapi.co：天气和大致位置
  • Resend：把客服留言转发到我们的邮箱
  • Apple：登录、付款、通知和 Apple 健康

这些服务只为我们提供上述功能而处理数据。其中一些位于欧洲经济区以外，这种情况下，数据传输依据欧盟标准合同条款等保障措施进行。

我们不会做的事

  • 不出售或出租你的数据。
  • 不展示广告，不使用广告追踪工具。
  • 不在其他公司的 App 或网站上追踪你。

法律依据（适用于欧盟和英国用户）

我们处理你的数据，是为了提供你要求的服务（合同履行）；对于 Apple 健康、运动与健身、通知等可选功能，基于你的同意；为保证 App 安全和正常运行，基于我们的合法利益。

保存多久

在你的账号存在期间保存。你删除账号后，我们会删除账号及其保存的数据，法律要求保留的除外。

你的选择和权利

  • 在 App 里删除账号：设置 → 我的账户 → 删除账户。
  • 随时在 iPhone「设置」里关闭 Apple 健康、运动与健身或通知权限。
  • 在「设置 → 隐私」里选择好友能看到什么。
  • 发邮件到 xicheninfo@gmail.com，要求获取、更正或删除你的数据。
  • 欧盟用户也可以向数据保护机构投诉。在比利时，是比利时数据保护局（Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données）。

儿童

枕边喵不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，我们不会有意收集他们的数据。如果你认为有儿童向我们提供了数据，请联系我们，我们会删除。

政策更新

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新上方的日期；如果是重要变化，会在 App 里通知你。
